# CircuitWorks Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How can I check the status of my order?
You can view your real-time order status and tracking information by logging into your CircuitWorks account and navigating to the 'Order History' page.

### 2. Do you offer bulk or wholesale pricing for large projects?
Yes! We offer tiered volume discounts on many components. If you need quantities higher than those listed on the product page, please contact our sales team for a custom quote.

### 3. Are your electronic components genuine?
Absolutely. We source 100% of our inventory directly from authorized manufacturers and reputable global franchised distributors. We do not source from unauthorized brokers.

### 4. Can I modify or cancel my order after it has been placed?
We process orders quickly to ensure fast delivery. If you need to make changes, please contact support immediately. Once an order enters the fulfillment stage, it can no longer be modified.

### 5. Do you provide datasheets and technical documentation?
Yes, direct links to official manufacturer datasheets are provided on every product page under the 'Downloads' tab.

### 6. How do I create a tax-exempt business or educational account?
Create a standard account first, then email your valid tax exemption certificate or institutional purchase documents to our support team before placing your order.

### 7. What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Net-30 terms for pre-approved institutional and corporate accounts, and wire transfers for large orders.

### 8. Why was my credit card declined during checkout?
Common reasons include mismatched billing addresses, expired cards, or triggered fraud protections from your bank. Please verify your details or contact your bank if the issue persists.

### 9. Do you ship to PO Boxes and APO/FPO addresses?
Yes, we ship via USPS to PO Boxes and military APO/FPO addresses. However, expedited courier services (FedEx/DHL) require a physical street address.

### 10. How should I handle ESD-sensitive parts when they arrive?
Always handle sensitive electronics like microcontrollers and bare ICs on an ESD-safe, grounded mat while wearing an ESD wrist strap to prevent latent static damage.
