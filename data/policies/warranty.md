# CircuitWorks Warranty Policy

CircuitWorks stands behind the quality of the electronics and robotics components we sell. 

## Warranty Length by Category
- **Semiconductors & Active Electronics:** 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects.
- **Passive Components (Resistors, Capacitors):** 90-day limited warranty.
- **Mechanical Parts (Motors, Actuators, Gearboxes):** 6-month limited warranty covering mechanical failure under normal operating loads.
- **Development Boards & Modules:** 1-year warranty.

## What Voids the Warranty
- Over-voltage, reverse polarity, or incorrect power supply connections.
- Electrostatic discharge (ESD) damage resulting from improper handling.
- Physical modification, soldering onto pins that were not intended for modification, or removal of heat sinks.
- Operation beyond specified environmental or electrical ratings (e.g., thermal overload).

## RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) Process
1. **Submit a Request:** Contact our support team with your order number, part number, and a detailed description of the issue.
2. **Troubleshooting:** Our technical team may ask you to perform basic troubleshooting steps.
3. **Issue RMA Number:** Once approved, you will receive an RMA number and shipping instructions.
4. **Inspection & Resolution:** Upon receipt, we will inspect the part and issue a replacement, store credit, or refund within 5 business days.
