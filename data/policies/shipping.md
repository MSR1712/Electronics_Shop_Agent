# CircuitWorks Shipping Policy

We strive to get your components into your lab as quickly and safely as possible.

## Processing Time
- In-stock orders typically process and ship within **1 to 2 business days**.
- Orders placed on weekends or major holidays will be processed on the next business day.

## Shipping Options & Tiers
- **Domestic (US):**
  - Economy (5-8 business days): Calculated at checkout based on weight.
  - Standard (3-5 business days): Flat rate available.
  - Expedited (2-3 business days) and Overnight options available at checkout.
- **International:**
  - We ship globally via DHL and FedEx Express.
  - Customers are responsible for all import duties, taxes, and customs clearance fees.

## Backordered Items
- If your order contains both in-stock and backordered items, you may choose at checkout whether to ship partial orders or wait for the entire order to be complete.
- Additional shipping charges may apply for split shipments.
