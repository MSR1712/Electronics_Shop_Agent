# CircuitWorks Returns Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your components. If you need to make a return, please review our guidelines below.

## Return Window
- You may return eligible items within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- Items must be postmarked within this 30-day window to qualify for a refund or exchange.

## Condition Requirements
- Items must be in their original, unopened packaging and in unused condition.
- Static-sensitive components (such as ICs and development boards) must remain in their original anti-static bags.
- Defective items may be returned outside standard conditions under our warranty policy.

## Restocking Fees
- Standard returns do not incur a restocking fee.
- Bulk returns or open-box items in sellable condition may be subject to a **15% restocking fee**.

## Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned under any circumstances:
- Cut-tape or custom-spooled wire/cable
- Custom-manufactured PCBs or assembled units
- Opened software licenses or digital downloads
- Clearance or final sale items
